Why we exist

Venture has an unusually informal pipeline. Much of what helps someone enter the industry — vocabulary, how investors think, what good judgment looks like, who to call, and where opportunities come from — is often learned through proximity.

This matters in an industry where women remain underrepresented and access isn't guaranteed. The gap isn't only visible on the cap table; in fact, it starts earlier, with who gets into the right rooms and is part of the right conversations.

What we do

There's no single way to learn venture. So we combine structured education with opportunities to practice, meet people doing the work, and eventually contribute to it yourself.

Learn it — a 10-week venture curriculum, from fund mechanics to cap tables to exits.

Apply it — small project teams working directly with early-stage founders on live business questions.

Engage with it — speaker sessions, the annual Summit, and industry visits.

Build it — friendships that extend well beyond a weekly meeting.

summit

Once a year, we bring our community together.

The Summit brings founders, investors, and operators together for candid conversations about the nuances of their careers — the wins, the mistakes, the uncertainty, and the parts of the work that rarely make it into a polished career story.

For us, the point isn't simply getting people into the right rooms. It's creating enough of them that access stops feeling exceptional.
