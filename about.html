what we do

Your way into venture.

There's no single way to learn venture. So we combine structured education with opportunities to practice, meet people doing the work, and eventually contribute to it yourself.

01

Learn it

Every new member completes a 10-week venture curriculum covering the mechanics behind investing — from how funds operate and evaluate to how a deal moves from first look to exit.

fund mechanics valuation cap tables term sheets due diligence deal memos portfolio management exits
02

Apply it

Members can earn their way onto small project teams working directly with early-stage founders on live business questions. Projects typically run for 8 weeks and move beyond hypothetical case studies into work founders can actually use — from investor research and fundraising materials to market mapping, growth, and go-to-market strategy.

For some members, those relationships have continued into formal internships.

see how project teams work →
03

Engage with it

Our speaker sessions, annual Summit, community events, mentorship program, and industry visits put members in conversation with founders, investors, and operators at different points in their careers.

04

Build it

Members learn together, build project teams together, exchange opportunities, ask each other questions, and form friendships that extend well beyond a weekly meeting.

why we exist

Access isn't guaranteed.

Venture has an unusually informal pipeline. Much of what helps someone enter the industry — vocabulary, how investors think, what good judgment looks like, who to call, and where opportunities come from — is often learned through proximity.

This matters in an industry where women remain underrepresented and access isn't guaranteed. The gap isn't only visible on the cap table; in fact, it starts earlier, with who gets into the right rooms and is part of the right conversations.
