community

We take the work seriously. Ourselves, slightly less so.

GIVC brings together women across majors, years, and interests who probably wouldn't all have ended up in the same room otherwise. What starts with learning venture quickly becomes project teammates, coffee chats, shared opportunities, long conversations after meetings, and friendships we genuinely value.

You might not arrive knowing exactly where you fit in venture — but you'll fit in the group, and you can figure out the rest alongside people doing the same.
