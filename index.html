Girls Into VC at UCLA aims to close the gender gap in venture capital by empowering the next generation of women. Through education, hands-on projects, and direct exposure to founders, we're building a more diverse and innovative VC ecosystem.

Who are we

Girls Into VC at UCLA is the first university chapter of Girls Into VC, founded in 2023 to create a meaningful entry point into venture at UCLA.

Through education, hands-on projects, and direct exposure to founders and investors, we turn an interest in venture into meaningful experiences.
