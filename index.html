who we are

Your way into venture.

Girls Into VC at UCLA is the first university chapter of Girls Into VC, founded in 2023 to create a meaningful entry point into venture for women at UCLA.

Through education, hands-on projects, and direct exposure to founders and investors, we turn an interest in venture into meaningful experiences.
