who we are

The first Girls Into VC chapter.

Girls Into VC at UCLA is the first university chapter of Girls Into VC, founded in 2023 to create a meaningful entry point into venture for women at UCLA.

Through education, hands-on projects, and direct exposure to founders and investors, we turn an interest in venture into something you can act on.

work with us

Project teams that ship real work.

Our project teams give members the opportunity to work directly with early-stage founders over the course of a quarter. Teams of four to six take ownership of defined strategic workstreams, produce formal deliverables, and learn what it means to be useful to a company in real time.

partner with us

Bring GIVC a problem.

Speak candidly with our members about your corner of the industry. Host us at your firm. Build an event together. Or simply start a conversation. We're always interested in meeting people who want to make the venture ecosystem easier to learn from — and who are willing to let talented students contribute to it.
