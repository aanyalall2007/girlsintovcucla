We partner with startups and venture firms on focused, quarter-long projects designed around real strategic questions.

For Startups

Designated teams work directly with early-stage founders on scoped projects across fundraising, market research, growth, and go-to-market strategy.

Market & Competitive Research

Market mapping, customer segments, competitor analysis, category positioning.

Go-To-Market Strategy

Customer acquisition research, channel strategy, launch planning, early GTM frameworks.

Brand Positioning

Messaging, competitive positioning, customer value proposition, and brand strategy.

Fundraising

Investor research, outreach tracking, pitch materials, data-room support, fundraising strategy.

For VC Firms

Dedicated teams support venture firms across sourcing, market mapping, thematic research, diligence, and portfolio company projects.

Sourcing & Market Mapping

Identify startups, founders, and emerging categories aligned with a fund's thesis.

Thematic Research

Deep dives into sectors, trends, emerging markets, or investment themes.

Diligence Support

Market sizing, competitor analysis, customer research, company benchmarking.

Portfolio Support

Market analysis, competitor research, growth research, and strategic support for portfolio companies.

Team Projects

Our team projects give members the opportunity to work directly with early-stage founders over the course of a quarter. Teams of four to six take ownership of defined strategic workstreams, produce formal deliverables, and learn what it means to be useful to a company in real time. For some members, those relationships have continued beyond the project into formal internships.
