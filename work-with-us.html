project teams

Work that founders can actually use.

Our project teams give members the opportunity to work directly with early-stage founders over the course of a quarter. Teams of four to six take ownership of defined strategic workstreams, produce formal deliverables, and learn what it means to be useful to a company in real time. For some members, those relationships have continued beyond the project into formal internships.
