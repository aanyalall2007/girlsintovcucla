work with us

For startups and VCs.

Meet thoughtful UCLA women early, put a project team to work on a real question, and build relationships with students who are serious about the ecosystem.

for startups

A project team on your fundraise.

Bring us a live business question — a fundraise, a market map, a go-to-market plan — and a team of four to six members will take ownership of it for a quarter, working directly with your team.

for vcs

A pipeline that shows up prepared.

Meet students who've completed a real venture curriculum before you ever interview them — plus opportunities to speak, host, and source directly from our chapter.

project teams

Work that founders can actually use.

Our project teams give members the opportunity to work directly with early-stage founders over the course of a quarter. Teams of four to six take ownership of defined strategic workstreams, produce formal deliverables, and learn what it means to be useful to a company in real time. For some members, those relationships have continued beyond the project into formal internships.
