Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience in venture capital, finance, or startups?

No. We welcome applicants with all levels of experience. We care more about curiosity, initiative, and a willingness to learn.

Do I need to be a business or economics major?

No. GIVC welcomes students from all majors and academic backgrounds.

What years can apply?

All UCLA undergraduate students are welcome to apply, regardless of year.

What does GIVC look for in an applicant?

We look for people who are curious, dependable, and excited to learn more about startups and the venture ecosystem. We value thoughtful questions, initiative, and a genuine willingness to contribute to the community.

What does the recruitment process look like?

Recruitment consists of a written application, a group coffee chat, and a final interview. Each stage is designed to help us get to know you better.

Do I need to attend the info session to apply?

No. Attendance is not required, but we highly recommend coming to learn more about GIVC, meet current members, and ask questions before applying.

I've applied to GIVC before. Can I apply again?

Absolutely. We encourage previous applicants to reapply and would love to see how you've grown since your last application.
